## Benchmark Comparison Narrative

### RAND Corporation

RAND reports typically feature quantified uncertainty, extensive appendix data, and peer review within the institution. The Flagship approaches RAND on **policy structure** and **risk matrices** but falls short on **primary data collection** and **classified/unclassified dual analysis** (not applicable here but expected for security topics).

### Brookings Institution

Brookings flagship reports combine accessible executive briefings with regional depth. The Flagship **matches** Brookings on executive briefing design and **approaches** regional analysis, but Brookings pieces typically include **named author expert credentials per chapter** and **interviews**—absent here.

### Chatham House

Chatham House excels at geopolitical balance and UK/EU/Global South voice. Flagship regional chapter improves Chatham alignment but **Middle East and ASEAN** sections need primary regional expert co-authorship for score ≥4.7.

### CSIS

CSIS emphasizes actionable timelines and stakeholder maps. Flagship Section 16 (Implementation Roadmap) **meets CSIS standards** on KPIs and budget phasing.

### SSCI-tier journals

SSCI requires original empirical contribution and methodological rigor. Flagship is **policy scholarship**, not journal article—appropriate for the series but would score 3.8–4.0 as a *Foreign Affairs*–length piece, not *International Organization*.

## Executive Assessment

The Flagship edition substantially addresses weaknesses in the working publication (15,000 words). The FMS Peace Architecture Framework™, regional policy analysis, expanded implementation roadmap, Author's Perspective, and FMS Vision 2050 chapters elevate the work toward think-tank flagship standards. **Residual weaknesses** center on mock LLM debate evidence, illustrative quantitative projections without econometric models, and limited primary empirical data on diplomatic AI pilots.

**Fixes applied in Flagship (not critique-only):** Framework chapter added; 6 regional perspectives; citation verification log; expanded budget/KPI tables; Vision 2050; Author's Perspective; McKinsey flagged as illustrative.
